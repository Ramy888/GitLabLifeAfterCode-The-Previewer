Delete your Time Tracker account

You can delete your account yourself, in the app or on the web, without reinstalling anything.

Steps

  1. Open the app, or the web version at https://time-tracker-web-57342278583.us-central1.run.app, and sign in.
  2. Open the Account tab and tap Delete account, then confirm.
  3. If the app asks you to sign in again, do so; Firebase requires a recent sign-in before an account can be deleted.

What is deleted

  • All your jobs and time entries are deleted first, immediately and permanently.
  • Then your sign-in account (email address or anonymous ID) is deleted.
  • Nothing is kept after deletion, and there are no backups we can restore from.

Settings stored on your device are removed when you uninstall the app.

Can't sign in?

Open an issue at https://gitlab.com/gitlab-ai-hackathon/transcend-october-2026/1947121/showcase/-/issues with the email address of the account (never your password) and we will delete it for you.
