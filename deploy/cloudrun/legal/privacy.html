Time Tracker privacy policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

Time Tracker lets you create jobs and log time entries against them. This page explains what data the app stores and what you can do about it.

What we store

  • Your sign-in account. If you sign in with email and password, Firebase Authentication stores your email address and a hashed password. If you sign in anonymously, it stores only a random user ID.
  • Your jobs and time entries. Job names, hourly rates, entry start and end times, and any comment you add. They are stored in Cloud Firestore under your user ID, and only your signed-in account can read or change them.
  • App settings on your device. A small amount of local state (for example, whether you finished onboarding) is kept on your device and never leaves it.

The app has no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We do not sell or share your data. Google provides Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore as our processors; data is stored in the United States.

How long we keep it

We keep your data until you delete it or delete your account. See Delete your account.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Contact

Privacy questions or requests: privacy@thepreviwer.dev. Other help: support. Never send a password.
